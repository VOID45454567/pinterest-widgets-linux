🖼️ Pinterest Widget for Linux

Bring your Pinterest boards and feed to your Linux desktop. A beautiful, customizable widget inspired by iOS/macOS widgets.

Features

  • Display your personal Pinterest feed on desktop
  • Show images from your favorite boards
  • Customizable grid layouts (2×2, 3×3, 4×4)
  • Auto-refresh with configurable intervals
  • Native Wayland/X11 support
  • Works on any Linux distribution

Privacy First

All your data stays on your device. We only request read-only access to your Pinterest boards and never post, modify, or share anything on your behalf.

Open Source

This project is open source and community-driven. Check out the code, contribute, or customize it for your needs.
